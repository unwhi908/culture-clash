# Sage — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Support (cleanser) | **HP:** 275
**Fantasy:** Native American herbalist. Warm, grounded, unshakeable — she doesn't panic, she makes tea. Completes the support trio: Nonna sustains through damage, Marquis punishes divers, Sage *erases debuffs*.

## Primary — Herbal Tea
Lobs a warm cup: allies in the splash get a heal-over-time (15 HP/s for 4s) and are cleansed of one debuff. Enemies take 15 damage. Drink your tea.

## Ability 1 — Spirit Totem (16s cooldown)
Places a totem (100 HP, destroyable) that pulses 20 HP/s to allies within 8m for 6s. The enemy has to decide: kill the totem or kill the team.

## Ability 2 — Cleansing Rain (14s cooldown)
A rain washes over allies within 10m: removes ALL debuffs and heals 40. The hard answer to Raj's Slurpee slow, Pierre's mime box, Marquis's Chair — the control meta's nightmare.

## Ultimate — Great Spirit's Blessing
Allies within 12m are cleansed, healed 150, and gain 30% damage reduction for 4s. The "we survive this teamfight" button. Shout: **"THE SPIRITS WATCH OVER US!"**

## Passive — The Earth Provides
Her heals-over-time tick 25% faster on allies below 50% HP. The earth gives most to those who need it.

## Voice lines
- "Drink your tea."
- "The earth provides."
- "Breathe."
- Ult: "THE SPIRITS WATCH OVER US!"

## Kit logic
Negate the enemy's control: cleanse the slows, the roots, the boxes, then outlast. Weak to raw burst and dives — cleansing doesn't help when Kenji is already behind you.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** control/debuff comps (Pierre, Raj's slow — her entire kit unwrites theirs)
- **Hunted by:** divers (Chen, Kenji — like every support), burst (Ivan's Big Game out-damages her HoTs)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
