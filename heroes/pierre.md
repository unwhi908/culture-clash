# Pierre — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Control | **HP:** 275
**Fantasy:** French mime. He says nothing, and that's the joke — his "voice lines" are silence and gestures. The most unique kit on the roster: he doesn't win fights, he decides *where* they happen.

## Primary — Pantomime Punch
Invisible punches, 35 damage, deceptively long 7m range. You can't see where it's coming from. That's the bit.

## Ability 1 — Invisible Wall (14s cooldown)
Places a 6m-wide wall for 4s — **invisible to enemies** (allies see a shimmer). Blocks movement *and* projectiles. Walk into it, bounce off it, get angry about it.

## Ability 2 — In a Box (12s cooldown)
Traps a single enemy in a mime box: 50% slow + can't use movement abilities for 2.5s.

## Ultimate — Grand Illusion
A giant invisible box cage (8m radius, 3.5s): enemies inside can't leave. They can still shoot — but they're not going anywhere.

## Passive — Living Statue
Takes 15% reduced damage while standing still. He commits to the bit.

## Voice lines
- "..." (silence)
- "Ooh la la!"
- Ult: a dramatic gasped inhale. The comedy is the silence.

## Kit logic
Dictate positioning: split teams, block escapes, ruin divers' days. Useless in a straight damage race — invaluable everywhere else.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** Master Chen, El Toro (walls stop dashes and charges cold; the cage wastes their engage)
- **Hunted by:** Raj (splash damage ignores walls), Bruce (outranges his short-range control)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
