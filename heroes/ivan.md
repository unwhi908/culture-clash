# Ivan — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Ranged DPS (sniper) | **HP:** 250
**Fantasy:** Russian Siberian hunter. A gentle giant who apologizes after every kill and is genuinely doing it for the winter stew. The precision answer to Bruce's angles and Raj's splash.

## Primary — Long Rifle
Charge-up shot: 40 damage uncharged, up to 110 fully charged. Slow, deliberate, devastating.

## Ability 1 — Bear Trap (14s cooldown)
Places a trap: first enemy to step in is rooted 2s and takes 30 damage. Watch your step.

## Ability 2 — Eagle Eye (16s cooldown)
Sees enemy outlines through walls for 3s. The hunter always knows where the prey is.

## Ultimate — Big Game
His next shot within 6s deals 250 damage and pierces through enemies. Shout: **"FOR THE WINTER STEW!"**

## Passive — Thick Coat
20% slow resistance. Siberian winters build character.

## Voice lines
- "Sorry about that."
- "For the stew!"
- Ult: "BIG GAME!"

## Kit logic
Hold sightlines, punish the static and the predictable. Helpless up close — the rifle needs distance, and everyone knows it.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** static targets (Hans's turret, Nonna's feast zone, anyone standing still)
- **Hunted by:** divers (Chen, Kenji, Lua — all three delete him up close)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
