# Bruce — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Ranged DPS | **HP:** 250
**Fantasy:** Australian. Everything he throws comes back — including, regrettably, his opinions.

## Primary — Boomerang
Throws a boomerang that flies 20m out and **returns**, dealing 30 damage on each pass. Angles are everything — the skill shot of the roster.

## Ability 1 — Ricochet (10s cooldown)
Next boomerang bounces between up to 3 enemies. Hallway-clearer.

## Ability 2 — Dingo Trap (14s cooldown)
Places a trap: the first enemy to step in is rooted for 1.5s. "Should've watched where you were going, mate."

## Ultimate — Barrage, Mate!
A fan of 5 large boomerangs, 60 damage per pass, all returning. Shout: **"BARRAGE, MATE!"**

## Passive — Comeback King
Return-pass hits deal +25% damage. The arc is the whole playstyle.

## Voice lines
- "G'day!"
- "That one's coming back!"
- Ult: "BARRAGE, MATE!"

## Kit logic
Poke from angles, punish static targets, control space with the trap. Weak the moment someone gets in his face — he has no escape button.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** Pierre (outranges his short control kit); pressures sustain comps with relentless poke
- **Hunted by:** Master Chen (Wok Block sends boomerangs home), El Toro (dives him — no escape)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
