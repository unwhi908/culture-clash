# Nonna Rosa — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Support | **HP:** 275
**Fantasy:** Italian chef. Nobody fights on an empty stomach — she keeps the whole family alive, one meatball at a time.

## Primary — Meatball Toss
Lobs arcing meatballs. Heals allies 40 on direct hit (20 splash), damages enemies 25. One button, two purposes — aim at whoever needs it most.

## Ability 1 — Extra Helping (12s cooldown)
Targeted burst heal: 110 HP to one ally. The save.

## Ability 2 — Mangia! (14s cooldown)
An ally gains 30% damage reduction for 3s and is cleansed of slows. "Eat, you'll feel better."

## Ultimate — Sunday Gravy
Places a feast zone (8m radius, 6s): allies inside heal 40/s and gain 15% damage. Shout: **"SUNDAY GRAVY!"** The whole team rallies around the table.

## Passive — Taste as You Go
Regenerates 12 HP/s after 4s without taking damage. She snacks while she works.

## Voice lines
- "Mangia, mangia!"
- "You look too skinny!"
- Ult: "SUNDAY GRAVY!"

## Kit logic
Win the attrition war — her sustain beats poke compositions. Positioning is everything: no mobility, so she lives or dies by her team peeling for her.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** poke/attrition (Raj's and Bruce's poke gets healed straight through)
- **Hunted by:** Master Chen (dives the backline and bursts her before help arrives)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
