# Hans — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Builder / Area Denial | **HP:** 300
**Fantasy:** German engineer, bratwurst enthusiast. Believes every problem can be solved with precision engineering and sausage. The roster's only builder — a whole new dimension of strategy.

## Primary — Nailgun
Ranged, 25 damage per shot, medium fire rate. For when the engineering needs a personal touch.

## Ability 1 — Wurst Turret (16s cooldown)
Deploys an auto-turret (150 HP, 20 dps) that looks suspiciously like a bratwurst stand. Holds ground, punishes the predictable.

## Ability 2 — Reinforce (12s cooldown)
Grants 100 temporary shield for 4s to an ally — or his turret. "Have you tried *not* breaking it?"

## Ultimate — Oktoberfest
8s overdrive: the turret fires at double rate, and allies within 10m gain +25% attack speed. Shout: **"OKTOBERFEST!"**

## Passive — German Engineering
Standing near his turret repairs it (15 HP/s); the turret has +25% max HP. He maintains his machines.

## Voice lines
- "Precision!"
- "Have a wurst."
- Ult: "OKTOBERFEST!"

## Kit logic
Set up the stand, dare the enemy to come. Wins fights he gets to prepare for; loses fights that come to him before he's set up.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** predictable pushes (the turret shreds anyone who walks in straight lines)
- **Hunted by:** Raj (splash damage melts the turret from safety), Kenji (dives Hans himself — kill the engineer, the stand goes quiet)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
