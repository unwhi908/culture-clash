# El Toro — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Tank | **HP:** 475
**Fantasy:** Luchador. A showman who fights for the roar of the crowd — massive, theatrical, unstoppable once moving.

## Primary — Clothesline
Wide melee swings, 55 damage, slow (0.8/sec). Clears space around him.

## Ability 1 — Lucha Charge (12s cooldown)
Charges 10m forward, dealing 60 damage and knocking back enemies hit. Initiation and disruption.

## Ability 2 — Suplex! (14s cooldown)
Grabs a nearby enemy and slams them: 80 damage + 1s stun. **Does not work on Master Chen** — Rooted Stance. The sifu cannot be suplexed, and El Toro hates it.

## Ultimate — From the Top Rope!
Leaps high to a targeted area and slams down: 120 damage + 1.5s stun in a 6m radius. Shout: **"FROM THE TOP ROPE!"** The clip-able moment.

## Passive — Thick Skin
Takes 15% reduced damage. He is simply hard to kill.

## Kit logic
Initiate fights, displace priority targets, soak damage while the team follows up. The anchor — every comp wants one, every comp must answer one.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** Raj, Bruce (dives and kills squishies); soft edge on Chen (wins the stat check, though Suplex fails)
- **Hunted by:** Pierre (walls stop charges cold, cage wastes his engage), Nonna Rosa (sustain outlasts his burst)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
