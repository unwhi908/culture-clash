# Lua — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Melee Skirmisher | **HP:** 275
**Fantasy:** Brazilian capoeirista. She doesn't fight so much as *dance at* people, and the dance has kicks. Never stops moving — stillness is death.

## Primary — Spinning Kick
Fast melee arc, 35 damage. Part of the dance.

## Ability 1 — Ginga (8s cooldown)
The core: a quick dash with 0.4s of evade (invulnerability). Low cooldown — this is how she lives.

## Ability 2 — Roda (12s cooldown)
A spinning AoE: 50 damage + knockback to everyone around her. The circle no one wants to enter.

## Ultimate — Carnaval
6s of festival: +40% move speed, +30% damage, Ginga cooldown halved. Shout: **"CARNAVAL!"**

## Passive — Rhythm
Moving builds stacks (up to 5); each stack grants +6% damage. Standing still drains them. The dance floor is everywhere.

## Voice lines
- "Axé!"
- "Dance with me!"
- Ult: "CARNAVAL!"

## Kit logic
Hit-and-run: dash in, kick, spin out before retaliation lands. Punishes slow, static targets; falls apart the moment she's pinned down.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** slow/static targets (Ivan, Hans, El Toro — too slow to catch the dance)
- **Hunted by:** Pierre (walls and cage pin her down), traps (Bruce's Dingo Trap, Ivan's Bear Trap, Marquis's Chair)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
