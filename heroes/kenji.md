# Kenji — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Melee Assassin | **HP:** 250
**Fantasy:** Japanese sushi chef and samurai. He treats combat the way he treats fish: with precision, respect, and extremely sharp knives. Everything is *omakase* — chef's choice.

## Primary — Fillet
Fast katana slashes, 25 damage each. Fast, light, relentless.

## Ability 1 — Quick Draw (10s cooldown)
Iaido dash-slash: dashes 8m through enemies, dealing 70 damage. The engage and the escape.

## Ability 2 — Omakase Roll (12s cooldown)
A dodge roll with 0.5s of invulnerability — the sushi roll. His next slash within 2s deals +50% damage.

## Ultimate — Thousand Cuts
Locks onto a target: 10 rapid slashes at 40 damage each over 2s. Shout: **"OMAKASE!"**

## Passive — Sharp Knife
Consecutive hits on the same target within 3s deal +10% each, stacking to +50%. Like slicing sashimi thinner and thinner.

## Voice lines
- "Omakase."
- "Fresh catch!"
- Ult: "THOUSAND CUTS!"

## Kit logic
Delete the backline: pick a squishy, Quick Draw in, roll the retaliation, Thousand Cuts the target. All-in by nature — if the burst doesn't kill, he's in trouble.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** backline (Nonna, Marquis, Ivan, Raj, Bruce — none of them survive the full combo)
- **Hunted by:** Pierre (the cage wastes his all-in), El Toro (can't burst 475 HP), Marquis (The Chair seats him mid-dash)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
