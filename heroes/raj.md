# Raj — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Ranged DPS | **HP:** 250 (squishy-ish)
**Fantasy:** 7-Eleven clerk by day, vigilante by night. The whole kit is a convenience-store food fight.

## Primary — Curry Throw
Lobs arcing globs of curry. Splash on impact.
- 45 damage direct hit / 25 splash
- Fire rate: ~1.2/sec, 8 globs per "tray", 1.5s reload (restocking)

## Ability 1 — Slurpee Freeze (10s cooldown)
Hurls a Slurpee cup that shatters into a frost zone (4m radius).
- 20 damage on shatter, 40% slow for 3s to enemies inside
- Your peel / setup tool: slow them, then land curry

## Ability 2 — Night Shift (14s cooldown)
Raj leans into the vigilante fantasy: turns invisible for 3s (breaks on attack).
- Next Curry Throw within 4s of breaking stealth deals +50% damage
- Engage, disengage, or set up a burst combo

## Ultimate — Spice Storm
Raj calls it in with **"MOTHER CHODE!"** — a barrage of flaming curry meteors over a targeted area.
- 12 meteors over 4s, 80 damage each in a small radius + burn (15/s for 2s)
- The teamfight-winning, clip-able moment

## Passive — Spice Tolerance
Burn and damage-over-time effects on Raj are 50% weaker. He's immune to his own spice.

## Voice lines
- On kill: "Thank you, come again!"
- Greeting: "Hello!"
- Ult activation: "MOTHER CHODE!"

## Kit logic
Poke (curry) → control space (slurpee) → reposition or burst (night shift) → win the teamfight (spice storm). Simple to learn, with a skill ceiling in the stealth-burst combo and ult placement.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** Pierre (splash damage ignores invisible walls); burn/DoT effects (Spice Tolerance)
- **Hunted by:** Master Chen (deflects curry with Wok Block, dives with Dumpling Dash), El Toro (too tanky to poke down, charges him)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
