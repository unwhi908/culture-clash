# Bjorn — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Tank (protector) | **HP:** 500
**Fantasy:** Norwegian Viking. Where El Toro is a showman who dives *into* the enemy, Bjorn is a wall who stands *in front of* his team. Skål!

## Primary — Axe
Heavy melee swings, 50 damage, slow. The axe does the talking.

## Ability 1 — Shield Wall (14s cooldown)
Raises a massive directional shield for 3s, blocking all frontal damage for everyone behind him. The team advances in his shadow.

## Ability 2 — War Horn (16s cooldown)
Allies within 12m gain +30% move speed and 50 temporary HP for 4s. Shout: **"FOR VALHALLA!"**

## Ultimate — Ragnarok
Leaps and slams: 100 damage + 1s stun in a 7m radius. Shout: **"RAGNAROK!"**

## Passive — Berserker
Gains +1% damage for every 2% missing HP (up to +40%). The lower he gets, the more dangerous he becomes. Finish him fast or don't start.

## Voice lines
- "For Valhalla!"
- "SKÅL!"
- Ult: "RAGNAROK!"

## Kit logic
Anchor the team: Shield Wall eats poke, War Horn starts pushes, Berserker punishes anyone who tries to grind him down. Useless if the fight goes around him — and it will try.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** poke comps (Shield Wall nullifies Raj's and Bruce's poke windows)
- **Hunted by:** Kenji (flanks around the shield — it only faces one way), Pierre (the cage ignores the wall entirely)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
