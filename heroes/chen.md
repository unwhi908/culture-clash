# Master Chen — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Melee Brawler | **HP:** 325
**Fantasy:** Retired kung fu sifu, dumpling-cart owner. Everything he does is kung fu — note: *kung fu*, the Chinese art. Precision, discipline, and a wok.

## Primary — Kung Fu Combo
Three-hit melee chain: 30 / 30 / 45. Fast, sticky, honest.

## Ability 1 — Dumpling Dash (10s cooldown)
Lunges 8m forward, dealing 40 damage to enemies passed through. Gap closer or escape.

## Ability 2 — Wok Block (12s cooldown)
Raises his wok for 1.5s, **deflecting incoming projectiles** back toward attackers. Each successful deflect refunds 2s on his other cooldowns. The anti-projectile tool — Raj's curry and Bruce's boomerangs come right back.

## Ultimate — Dragon's Fury
A 3s flurry: 8 strikes at 55 damage each in a 5m radius around him. Shout: **"DRAGON'S FURY!"**

## Passive — Rooted Stance
Immune to knockback and displacement (El Toro's Suplex does nothing to him). A rooted sifu cannot be moved.

## Voice lines
- "Ah, a worthy student!"
- "Have a dumpling!"
- Ult: "DRAGON'S FURY!"

## Kit logic
Dive the backline, deflect the poke, stick to targets. The duelist — unbeatable in a fair fight, so don't give him one.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** Raj, Bruce (Wok Block turns their projectiles against them; Dash closes the gap)
- **Hunted by:** Pierre (walls block dash paths, cage traps him), El Toro (can't burst 475 HP through Thick Skin — loses the stat check)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
