# Marquis — Kit Design (v1 LOCKED 2026-10-13)

**Role:** Support (anti-dive) | **HP:** 275
**Fantasy:** Black American barber. The shop is sacred ground — everybody leaves feeling like a million bucks, and anybody who starts trouble takes a seat. Designed as Nonna's opposite: where she sustains through poke, he *punishes divers*.

## Primary — Hot Lather
Lobs shaving-cream dollops. Heals allies 35 on direct hit (15 splash), damages enemies 20. One button, two purposes.

## Ability 1 — The Chair (14s cooldown)
"Take a seat." Forces a single enemy to sit: rooted 1.5s in a barber chair that appears under them. The anti-dive button — Chen mid-Dumpling-Dash, Kenji mid-Quick-Draw, all seated.

## Ability 2 — Fresh Cut (14s cooldown)
An ally gets a crisp lineup: +25% damage for 4s. Confidence is a buff.

## Ultimate — Shop Talk
Marquis holds court for 6s: allies in a 10m radius heal 30/s and gain +15% damage. Everybody's feeling themselves. Shout: **"SHOP'S OPEN!"**

## Passive — Regulars
Allies recently healed or buffed by Marquis gain +10% move speed for 3s. You walk out of his chair *lighter*.

## Voice lines
- "Take a seat!"
- "You needed this."
- "Who's next?!"
- Ult: "SHOP'S OPEN!"

## Kit logic
Bodyguard the backline: sit divers down, buff your carries, hold court when the fight breaks out. Weak when poked from range — he has to stand near the fight to work.

## Counters
- **Preys on:** divers (Chen, Kenji, Lua — The Chair ends their engage)
- **Hunted by:** ranged poke (Raj, Bruce, Ivan — he can't answer damage from 20m)

*Numbers are v1 — everything gets tuned in playtesting (Phase 2+).*
